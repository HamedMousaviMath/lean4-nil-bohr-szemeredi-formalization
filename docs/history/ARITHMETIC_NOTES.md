## Scalar Heisenberg external input

`ScalarHeisenbergObstruction` is a proposition parameter shared by the scalar deductions, with positive constants C,D universal for fixed geometry. For all real α,β, starting points a∈ℤ, integer steps v>0 and dilations s>0, K>1, lattice-invariant tests with sup-plus-Lipschitz norm at most U, and 0<δ<1/2, discrepancy strictly greater than δU implies integers k,l with `0 < max(|k|,|l|) ≤ C δ^(−D)` and `K ‖v(kα+lβ)/s‖_T ≤ C δ^(−D)`. No Diophantine restrictions are imposed by this premise.

Reference: Green–Tao, *The quantitative behaviour of polynomial orbits on nilmanifolds*, arXiv:0709.3562, Theorem 2.9 and Definitions 1.2(iii), 2.2, 2.6, 2.7. The generalized theorem concludes a horizontal character with a polynomially bounded modulus and smoothness norm under a bounded-rationality adapted Mal’cev basis. The interface uses the right lattice classes in Heisenberg matrix coordinates, chain and quotient distance formulas, and integration over a unit coordinate chart. It is a specialization, including a chart/quotient bridge. Comparison of that coordinate metric with the actual quotient topology, identification of the chart integral with Haar probability, filtered polynomial membership and identification of all horizontal characters with integer linear horizontal coordinates remain external along with Green–Tao's estimate. Formula for the linear coefficient after dilation is checked separately. No product-circle obstruction is assumed or proved by this interface.
