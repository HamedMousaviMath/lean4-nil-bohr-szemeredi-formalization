1 Definitions and dependency graph

Definition 1 Selector and shifted-prime set
#

For integer \(n\) put

\[ Z(n)=\frac{\alpha \beta }{2}n(n-1)-\alpha n\lfloor \beta n\rfloor , \qquad Z_{\mathrm{br}}(n)=\alpha (n+1)\lfloor \beta (n+1)\rfloor . \]

Here \(\inf _{m\ge 1}m\lVert m\alpha \rVert _{\mathbb T}{\gt}0\). The number \(\beta \in (0,1)\) has increasing reduced prime-denominator approximants \(a_j/r_j\) with \(r_j\to \infty \), \(r_{j+1}\asymp \exp (\exp (\sqrt{\log r_j}))\) and \(0{\lt}\beta -a_j/r_j\ll r_{j+1}^{-1}\). For an open arc \(J\subset \mathbb T=\mathbb R/\mathbb Z\) with \(0{\lt}|J|{\lt}1\), \(\mathcal B_{Z,J}=\{ n\in \mathbb Z_{{\gt}0}:Z(n)\bmod 1\in J\} \). The shifted-prime selectors are \(\mathcal P_{V,J}=\{ p\in \mathbb P:V(p-1)\bmod 1\in J\} \) for \(V=Z\) or \(Z_{\mathrm{br}}\). Write \(\pi _{V,J}(N)=|\mathcal P_{V,J}\cap [N]|\).

Definition 2 Rescaled counting and phase tests
#

Write \([M]=\{ 1,\ldots ,M\} \), \(P_0=0\), and fix integral polynomials \(P_i(0)=0\) of distinct increasing positive degrees with maximum \(d\). For a positive integer \(q\), the shift \(P_{i,q}(y)=P_i(qy)/q\) is integral. A degree-\(e\) polynomial with zero constant term has \((C,q)\)-coefficients if its leading coefficient is \(a'_e q^{e-1}\) with \(0{\lt}|a'_e|\le C\) and every lower nonconstant coefficient has absolute value at most \(C\) times that of the leading coefficient. This class includes all \(P_{i,q}\) for some fixed \(C\).

For any such family \(R_i\), \(R_0=0\), and \(1\)-bounded spatial inputs of finite support, the test count and polynomial-phase norm are

\[ T_{N,M,v}(\mathbf f) =\frac{1}{NM}\sum _{x\in \mathbb Z}\sum _{y\in [M]} v(y)\prod _{i=0}^{k-1}f_i(x+R_i(y)),\qquad \lVert v\rVert _{u^s[P]} =\sup _{\deg \phi {\lt}s}\lvert \mathbb E_{y\in P}v(y)\mathrm e(-\phi (y))\rvert . \]

The tests use real polynomials, \(P\) is a nonempty finite progression, and \(\mathrm e(t)=e^{2\pi it}\). For a nonempty finite interval or progression \(I\), the full \(U^s[I]\) norm takes the alternating conjugation cube sum of the zero extension and divides the resulting norm by that of \(\mathbf1_I\). The normalized prime weight is \(\Lambda _q(y)=(\varphi (q)/q)\Lambda (qy+1)\), and \(F_q(y)=F(Z(qy))\) with \(\mu _F=\int _\mathbb TF\).
