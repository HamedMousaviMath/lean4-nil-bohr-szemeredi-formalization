Definition 2Rescaled counting and phase tests

Write \([M]=\{ 1,\ldots ,M\} \), \(P_0=0\), and fix integral polynomials \(P_i(0)=0\) of distinct increasing positive degrees with maximum \(d\). For a positive integer \(q\), the shift \(P_{i,q}(y)=P_i(qy)/q\) is integral. A degree-\(e\) polynomial with zero constant term has \((C,q)\)-coefficients if its leading coefficient is \(a'_e q^{e-1}\) with \(0{\lt}|a'_e|\le C\) and every lower nonconstant coefficient has absolute value at most \(C\) times that of the leading coefficient. This class includes all \(P_{i,q}\) for some fixed \(C\).

\[ T_{N,M,v}(\mathbf f) =\frac{1}{NM}\sum _{x\in \mathbb Z}\sum _{y\in [M]} v(y)\prod _{i=0}^{k-1}f_i(x+R_i(y)),\qquad \lVert v\rVert _{u^s[P]} =\sup _{\deg \phi {\lt}s}\lvert \mathbb E_{y\in P}v(y)\mathrm e(-\phi (y))\rvert . \]

The tests use real polynomials, \(P\) is a nonempty finite progression, and \(\mathrm e(t)=e^{2\pi it}\). The normalized prime weight is \(\Lambda _q(y)=(\varphi (q)/q)\Lambda (qy+1)\), and \(F_q(y)=F(Z(qy))\) with \(\mu _F=\int _\mathbb TF\).
